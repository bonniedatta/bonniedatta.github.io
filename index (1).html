About

I've spent 15+ years in finance at high-growth tech companies including PayPal, Workday, SailPoint, Twitter and Upwork. My work translates business models into financial plans, sets up and maintains P&Ls and KPIs, and partners closely with the business to drive growth while managing cash and margins.
